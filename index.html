Room codeBoth players pick a fighter in the P1 slot. The host picks the map and starts. Your friend must be invited to this page as a Contributor or higher (Viewers and public links can’t connect). Each player uses the P1 keys on their own device.

P1: A/D move · W jump (double jump) · S fast-fall / drop through · F attack · G special
P2: ← → move · ↑ jump · ↓ fast-fall · . attack · / special
3 normal attacks: Attack = forward, Up+Attack = launcher, Down+Attack = low sweep. Special = signature move with a visible cooldown bar (under your feet and on your HUD card). Up+Special is a short-cooldown recovery leap (once per airtime).
Combos: land a hit, then press Attack again (any direction) to chain up to 4 hits. The 4th is a launching FINISHER. You can also cancel a hit into your Special. Hits landed in quick succession build a combo meter that adds bonus damage. Esc or P pauses.
Defense: hold Shield (P1: H, P2: comma) to block. Shield + a direction rolls or air-dodges. Shield + Attack grabs and throws (beats shields). Final Smash: hits fill the gold meter under your HUD card; at 100% press the Final Smash key (P1: T, P2: semicolon) for a screen-wide blast. Items drop in: heart heals 25%, star adds meter, bomb is a trap. Hazards: Skyline has lightning storms, Twin Peaks has lava geysers. Modes: Team Battle (P1+P3 vs P2+P4) and Training.
Extras: every fighter has a unique-named Final Smash (T / semicolon). Taunt: R (P1) or quote (P2). You grab ledges automatically when you fall near a main platform edge; press Jump to climb up or Down to drop. Duplicate characters get a recolor. The camera zooms in when fighters get close, and the last KO plays in slow motion. Gamepads: pad 1 = P1, pad 2 = P2 (stick or D-pad move, A jump, X attack, B special, Y Final Smash, LB taunt, RB or RT shield).
